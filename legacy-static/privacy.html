Strict Zero-Logging Commitment

We do not log DNS queries or browsing activity. We never monitor, store, profile, sell, or disclose the domains you visit, games you connect to, or web traffic routed through our Anycast DNS resolvers. Queries are resolved strictly in temporary RAM buffers and discarded instantaneously.

1. Information We Collect

At YourBrand, we practice rigorous data minimization. We only collect the minimal personal information required to authenticate your subscription and deliver premium technical support:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, and WhatsApp mobile phone number provided during checkout or support tickets;
  • Order & Billing Data: Transaction ID (TID), chosen plan tier, payment method (Easypaisa or JazzCash), and timestamp of purchase;
  • Device Metadata: High-level operating system type (iOS or Android) if provided voluntarily to supply customized setup configuration files;
  • Aggregated Server Metrics: Total query volume, cache hit ratios, and server load percentages. These metrics are strictly non-attributable and cannot be traced back to individual accounts or IP addresses.

2. How We Use Information

The limited personal information collected is utilized exclusively for genuine operational purposes:

  • To provision and activate your dedicated private DNS hostnames or profile configurations;
  • To verify transaction receipts and prevent fraudulent payment submissions;
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot connection issues via WhatsApp or email;
  • To dispatch subscription expiry and renewal reminders (opt-out available at any time);
  • To detect and counter abusive denial-of-service attempts against our DNS resolver nodes.

We do not use your personal information for automated behavioral profiling, nor do we run cross-site tracking scripts or sell user lists to marketing aggregators.

3. DNS Query Privacy (Our Core Architecture)

We do not log DNS queries or browsing activity under any circumstances. Our recursive resolver infrastructure runs on stateless in-memory operating systems configured specifically to disable disk query logging (query logging is piped to /dev/null).

When your mobile device queries our DNS servers (for example, to connect to game matchmaking or patch servers), our resolver retrieves the corresponding IP address from root and authoritative servers, returns the answer to your device, and purges the query context from volatile memory.

We do not inspect packet payloads, inspect SNI certificates, inject advertisements, or redirect failed lookups to commercial affiliate portals.

4. Payment Information & Mobile Wallets

We process transactions primarily through Pakistani digital wallet services such as Easypaisa (Telenor Microfinance Bank) and JazzCash (Mobilink Microfinance Bank). You make payments directly within your banking application or mobile wallet interface.

YourBrand never receives, processes, or stores your banking PINs, OTP codes, credit card numbers, or mobile wallet login passwords. We only retain the transaction receipt reference (TID) to confirm the payment amount and sender mobile number.

All checkout submissions sent through our website are secured using industry-standard TLS 1.3 cryptographic encryption.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data to advertisers, telemetry companies, or external brokers. We share data only in strictly bounded operational scenarios:

  • Essential Infrastructure Partners: Cloud and data center providers hosting our server nodes (under confidentiality and zero-logging operational agreements);
  • Legal Compliance: If compelled by an official, binding court order issued by a court of competent jurisdiction under Pakistani law. Because we do not keep DNS query logs, we have no query history or browsing data to provide even if formally requested.

6. Data Security & Storage Integrity

We take comprehensive administrative and technical measures to safeguard customer account records from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. These protections include:

  • End-to-end TLS encryption for all website communications;
  • Restricted, key-based SSH administrative access to server clusters;
  • Encrypted backup storage for subscriber status registries;
  • Continuous intrusion detection and rate-limiting safeguards.

While no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% impenetrable, we employ defense-in-depth methodologies aligned with international cybersecurity standards.

7. Your Rights & Data Control

You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data. Depending on your location and applicable privacy regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access & Portability: Request a complete summary of the account details we maintain regarding your subscription;
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate phone numbers or email addresses associated with your order;
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your customer record and contact details from our active records once your subscription term concludes.

To exercise any of these privacy rights, simply reach out to our privacy officer via our contact channels.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for mobile gamers and general internet users who are at least 13 years of age (or the minimum legal age required to consent in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

If we become aware that personal information of a child under 13 has been submitted without verified consent, we will promptly delete such information from our records.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements in our network architecture, new features, or evolving regulatory requirements. The latest version will always be accessible on this page with the updated revision timestamp.

Substantial revisions affecting how we handle subscriber contact records will be announced with advance notice via our website banner or WhatsApp broadcast notification.
