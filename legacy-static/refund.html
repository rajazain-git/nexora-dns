1. Refund Eligibility

At YourBrand, customer satisfaction and fair business ethics are fundamental. You qualify for a 100% full refund under the following conditions:

  • Time Window: Your refund request is submitted within 3 calendar days (72 hours) from the exact moment your payment was confirmed;
  • Unused Credentials: The assigned private DNS hostname or resolver credentials have not been actively connected to high-volume routing or verified server sessions;
  • Technical Incompatibility: In cases where your ISP (Internet Service Provider) actively blocks private DoH/DoT configurations or your device operating system does not support custom DNS routing, and our technical agents cannot provide a working workaround.

We review all requests fairly and will never keep your money if our service genuinely failed to perform on your setup.

2. How to Request a Refund

We do not require complicated paperwork or endless support tickets. To initiate a refund request, follow these straightforward steps:

  1. Prepare your verification details: Have your Order ID (e.g. YB-104829), the mobile phone number used during checkout, and the transaction screenshot or Transaction ID (TID) from Easypaisa or JazzCash.
  2. Contact our billing team via WhatsApp: WhatsApp is our fastest channel for financial transactions. Message our official support line with the text "Refund Request for Order [Your Order ID]".
  3. Briefly explain the reason: Share a quick note about why the DNS didn't work for your needs (e.g., unsupported cellular network, changed mind before activation, or ping didn't improve on your ISP). This helps us improve our network routes.

Alternatively, if you prefer email, you may send your request to support@yourbrand.com with the subject line "Refund Request - [Order ID]".

3. Processing Time & Payout Methods

Once our billing team confirms your eligibility, your refund will be processed immediately according to these timelines:

  • Review & Verification: Usually completed within 2 to 6 hours during regular operating hours (10:00 AM – 12:00 AM PKT);
  • Payout Execution: Funds will be transferred back to your original source account (Easypaisa wallet or JazzCash wallet) within 24 to 48 business hours;
  • Transaction Proof: You will receive a direct transaction screenshot with the newly generated reversal TID via WhatsApp for your records.

Please note that banking network maintenance or wallet maintenance windows may occasionally cause minor inter-bank clearing delays.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

While we aim to be as accommodating as possible, refunds cannot be granted in the following scenarios:

  • Requests Submitted Past 72 Hours: Any inquiry submitted after the initial 3-day guarantee window has lapsed;
  • Substantial Usage: Accounts that have actively used our high-speed routing servers for competitive tournaments, multiple gaming sessions, or heavy data consumption over more than 3 days;
  • Violations of Terms of Service: Users whose accounts were banned or suspended for DDoS attacks, scraping, botnet activity, or sharing private hostnames publicly;
  • In-Game Actions: Game bans, third-party cheat software penalties, or account restrictions imposed by game publishers (such as Tencent, Garena, or Activision). Our DNS only resolves addresses and does not modify game memory or game files;
  • Subsequent Renewal Cycles: Subscriptions that have already completed their initial billing term.
